You open Google Maps to walk from your hotel to a restaurant, and it just… can’t. No walking directions. No transit routes. No subway exit numbers.

If this has happened to you in Korea, you’re not missing a setting — Google Maps genuinely can’t do most of that here, and almost everyone, tourists included, switches to Naver Map instead.

Naver has also been actively closing the gaps that used to make Korean apps hard for foreign visitors, most recently by letting you verify your identity with just a passport — no Korean phone number required.

Why Doesn’t Google Maps Work in Korea?

South Korea restricts the export of detailed mapping data for national security reasons, and Google has never been granted the license it needs to build full turn-by-turn navigation here. The result: Google Maps can show you pins and reviews, but it drops walking directions, driving routes, and public transit directions the moment you actually try to move somewhere. That gap is exactly what local map apps exist to fill.

⚠️ What Google Maps can’t do in Korea
No walking directions between two points. No driving/turn-by-turn navigation. No bus or subway routing, transfer info, or real-time arrival times. It’s fine for looking up a place’s name, address, or opening hours — just don’t rely on it to get you there.

What Is Naver Map?

Naver Map is a free app built by Naver, the company behind Korea’s most-used search engine and the Papago translator. Naver itself describes it as an all-in-one platform for discovering places, getting personalized recommendations, and finding the best routes across Korea.

In practice, that means walking, driving, and cycling directions, full public transit routing with live bus and subway arrival times, subway exit numbers down to the digit, indoor AR navigation inside large buildings and malls, and business hours and reviews for almost anywhere you’d search.

It’s the app locals actually use, which also means it’s the one with the most accurate, up-to-date information. It’s available on the App Store, Google Play, and as a website at map.naver.com.

✅ Bottom line
If you’re getting around Korea on foot, by subway, or by bus, Naver Map isn’t optional the way a map app might be in other countries — it’s the tool almost every trip depends on.

New: Passport Verification Makes It Easier for Foreigners

One of the biggest hidden obstacles for foreign travelers using Korean apps has always been the same thing: most useful features are locked behind a Korean phone number. As of June 2026, Naver has started removing that barrier for its map app.

With Passport Verification, you can now confirm your identity using just your passport, directly on your phone, with no Korean mobile number and no need to contact customer support.

Once verified, that same identity unlocks NAVER Booking, NAVER Order, and Naver Pay inside the map app — so you can reserve a table, order ahead, and pay, all from the app you’re already using to find the place.

Naver has also been expanding language support in phases: user reviews on Naver Map are already translated into English, Chinese, and Japanese, and a multilingual Naver Map Guide has provided travel guidance since December 2024.

ℹ️ Why this matters for your trip
Needing a Korean phone number used to be the point where a lot of useful Korean apps stopped being usable for short-term visitors. Passport verification is Naver directly addressing that — it’s worth checking for the option next time you’re prompted to verify your identity in the app.

How Do You Download Naver Map?

  1. Search “Naver Map” (or “NAVER Map”) in the App Store on iPhone, or Google Play on Android — or use the download buttons above.
  2. Install it like any other app; no Korean phone number or account is required to use the core map and navigation features.
  3. Open the app once before your trip and allow location access, so it’s ready to use the moment you land.

How Do You Switch Naver Map to English?

Naver Map ships with an English interface, but it doesn’t always default to it depending on your phone’s region settings. Switching it over takes under a minute:

  1. Open the Naver Map app and tap the menu icon (the three lines, usually in a corner of the main screen).
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Find the Language option and select English.
  4. The app switches immediately — menus, search results, and turn-by-turn directions will now show in English.

ℹ️ Search still works in English
Even before switching, you can usually search for well-known place names in English (hotel names, landmarks, station names) and Naver Map will find the right result — it’s the interface labels that need the language switch.

How Do You Actually Use Naver Map?

Once it’s installed and set to English, using Naver Map day-to-day is straightforward:

  • Search by name or address — type a landmark, restaurant, or hotel name and pick it from the results, then tap Directions.
  • Pick your mode — walking, driving, or public transit (bus + subway combined), each with its own estimated time and step-by-step route.
  • Check subway exits — Naver Map tells you the exact exit number to take, which matters at large transfer stations with a dozen-plus exits.
  • Read reviews in your language — many listings show reviews translated into English, Chinese, or Japanese, so you don’t need to guess what a Korean-only review says.
  • Save places you plan to visit — bookmark them before your trip so you’re not re-searching the same address every day.
  • Download offline maps for your region if you’re worried about data — useful backup if you’re relying on a limited SIM card or eSIM data plan.

Beyond Navigation: Booking, Ordering & Local Discovery

Naver Map isn’t just a navigation app — it’s the front door to Naver’s wider local business ecosystem, called NAVER Place.

Once you’ve searched for a restaurant or café, the same listing often lets you check real-time business hours, browse photos and videos from verified visitors, join a waitlist or make a reservation through NAVER Booking, or order ahead for pickup through NAVER Order — all without leaving the app, and now reachable by foreign visitors through passport verification instead of a Korean phone number.

Naver also periodically runs campaigns aimed specifically at foreign travelers to surface spots beyond the usual tourist strip.

As one example, its 2026 “BE LOCAL” campaign — run with Busan Metropolitan City and the Korea Tourism Organization — curated roughly 300 neighborhood spots across Seoul and Busan (including Haeundae, Seomyeon, and Gijang) behind a dedicated tab for non-Korean-language app users, with discount coupons at local restaurants, including some MICHELIN-selected ones.

That particular campaign has since ended, but it’s a good sign of where Naver is investing: Naver reported a 16.4% jump in users with a non-Korean language setting (April 2026 vs. September 2025), and says more region- and language-specific features are on the way. It’s worth glancing at the app’s home tab for a currently-running promotion before you assume there isn’t one.

FAQ: Naver Map for Travelers

Is Naver Map available in English?

Yes. Switch it under the app’s menu → Settings → Language → English. Place search works in English even before you switch, and many reviews are already translated into English, Chinese, and Japanese.

Is Naver Map free?

Yes, it’s free to download and use, with no Korean phone number or payment required for navigation.

Do I need a Korean phone number to use Naver Map’s other features?

Not anymore for identity verification — Naver introduced passport verification in June 2026, letting foreign visitors verify their identity without a Korean mobile number to unlock booking, ordering, and payment features.

Do I need Naver Map if I already have Google Maps?

For most trips, yes. Google Maps can’t provide walking, driving, or transit directions inside Korea, so you’ll want Naver Map (or Kakao Map) as your actual navigation app.

Naver Map or Kakao Map — which should I pick?

Start with Naver Map for its more complete English support. Add Kakao Map later only if you find yourself wanting its subway diagrams as a second opinion.

Does Naver Map work offline?

You can download maps for offline use, which is a useful backup if you’re on a limited data plan, though live transit arrival times and features like Booking or Order need a connection.
