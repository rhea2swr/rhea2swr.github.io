1330 Travel Helpline

1330 is Korea Tourism Organization's free, multilingual hotline for travel information, interpretation, and tourist complaints — available 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Call from inside KoreaDial 1330Call from outside Korea+82-2-1330Chat with 1330No app download needed

Prefer a free online call, or chatting through LINE, Facebook Messenger, or WhatsApp instead? Visit the official 1330 Travel Helpline page for those options.

Need to report a tourist complaint? Visit touristcomplaint.or.kr.
